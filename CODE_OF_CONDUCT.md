# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Blink to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good community:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and other contributions that do not
follow this code, and will explain their decision when appropriate.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the
maintainer through GitHub ([@iammohitraj](https://github.com/iammohitraj)). Reports are
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private warning, a temporary
ban from interaction, then a permanent ban from the project.
